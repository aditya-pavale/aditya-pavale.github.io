AI visibility checking: frequently asked questions

These are short answers to common questions about checking a brand's visibility in Google results and AI assistant answers.

How many questions do I need?

Start with about 100 real questions that creators or customers ask, from recorded sources, and add more over time.

Why store the raw answer?

Stored raw answers let anyone re-check a score and prove it was not typed by hand.

Should I check once or several times?

Check at least twice on different days, because AI answers vary.

What if an assistant shows no sources?

Record that Cited cannot be detected for it, and score Mentioned only.

Does location matter?

Yes. Record the location setting for every check, and measure more than one market.
